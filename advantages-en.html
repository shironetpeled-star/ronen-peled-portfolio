My Advantages

A multidisciplinary combination of needs analysis, systems thinking, technology, management and genuine customer partnership — through to a working solution that creates value.

🎯Understanding needs, users and business value

  • Understanding the real need and the right solution — understanding what to build, why, for whom and which value the solution must create; assessing customer needs, system capabilities and the path to production.
  • Focus on users and business value — balancing user needs, company objectives and technical capabilities, adapting the system to organizational and user needs rather than adapting the needs to system limitations.
  • Marketing and business perspective — combining digital-marketing training and direct customer experience to understand the market, customer needs, the value proposition and Go-to-Market.
  • Deep customer understanding — years in service, support and Help Desk have given me a deep understanding of customer needs, thinking, difficulties and expectations of service and systems.

🧠Analytical thinking and complex problem solving

  • Analytical thinking and opportunity identification — identifying patterns, gaps and opportunities and making informed decisions.
  • Root Cause Analysis — going beyond an inquiry or symptom to understand why the problem occurred, what needs to change and how to address the underlying cause.
  • Complex problem solving — assessing alternatives, benefits, drawbacks and impacts to find the most complete, precise and feasible solution.
  • Creativity and thinking outside the box — finding new and alternative solutions when the usual approach does not provide an appropriate answer.

🌐Systems and multidisciplinary perspective

  • Systems and organizational perspective — understanding the entire business and organizational process and connecting processes, systems, data and information from different sources, beyond code or development alone.
  • A multidisciplinary 360° perspective — combining hands-on development, product, system-analysis and customer experience to assess requirements from technical, business and human perspectives.
  • A cross-functional view of work processes — practical familiarity with diverse roles and processes and their effects on one another throughout the product and system lifecycle.
  • Experience across systems and industries — practical familiarity with different systems and business domains, helping identify patterns, quickly understand new processes and adapt solutions to organizational and user needs.
  • Early identification of system-wide impact — identifying needs, constraints, interfaces, dependencies and potential effects before development begins.

🔗Connecting business and technology

  • Bridging business and technology — translating business needs and requirements into technical capabilities and the specification, planning and development of a feasible solution.
  • Connecting the customer, product and development — translating customer needs for the relevant professionals, creating a shared language and coordinating everyone through solution definition, development and implementation.
  • 360° system analysis — assessing requirements from technical, business and human perspectives, informed by practical experience across the full product and system lifecycle.
  • A complete and precise systems solution — creating a solution suited to organizational, customer and user needs while accounting for technical capabilities, existing processes and business objectives.

🧭End-to-end leadership and management

  • Ownership and an end-to-end perspective — combining product, technology, system analysis and Customer Success, from the initial need through PRDs, prototype management, development, QA and A/B testing to implementation, validation and continuous improvement.
  • 360° project management — leading projects through a combined assessment of technical, business and human aspects.
  • Start to Finish — guiding a system or feature from the initial request and business need through specification, planning, development and testing to launch, actual use and verification of the required value.
  • Complete delivery while working remotely — during five of my 15 years in the role, I worked remotely across the full product lifecycle, from business and development aspects through direct customer work, except for tasks requiring physical presence.
  • Stakeholder management — understanding and coordinating the needs of all stakeholders, including users, customers, managers and professional contributors, throughout the project.
  • Managing tasks, interfaces and dependencies across parallel projects — prioritizing across projects using a clear task matrix defining tasks, ownership, interfaces and dependencies for each project.
  • Early identification of obstacles and risks — identifying bottlenecks, friction, ownership gaps, dependencies and risks before they delay the project.
  • Removing obstacles and maintaining progress — actively resolving obstacles, connecting relevant contributors and maintaining steady progress through full implementation.

💻Development, systems and integrations

  • Extensive MAGIC experience — hands-on experience with many MAGIC versions over the years in development, maintenance, upgrades and system expansion.
  • Building new systems end to end — building new systems from specification and PRDs through prototype management, planning, development, QA and A/B testing to implementation and a working production system.
  • Development in live systems — maintaining, modifying and extending existing systems while rebuilding and implementing features within a live system.
  • Data, interfaces and integrations — transferring data and developing interfaces and integrations across systems, software and information sources.
  • Migrations and upgrades — data migrations, system transitions and version upgrades while maintaining operational continuity and alignment with business needs.
  • Delivery and implementation — testing, implementation, production launch and support for actual use.

🤝Service, guidance and customer partnership

  • Personal, continuous and proactive guidance — close customer guidance from the initial need or problem through investigation, handling, stakeholder coordination, resolution and implementation, ensuring satisfaction and value.
  • Continuous and transparent communication — keeping the customer and other stakeholders informed, coordinated and clear about expectations throughout the process.
  • Building trust and customer partnership — creating long-term relationships based on reliability, transparency and a deep understanding of customer needs, strengthening the customer’s partnership and confidence in the company and its solution.
  • Ownership through complete resolution — connecting the customer, product and development, addressing the root cause and guiding the process through complete resolution and successful use rather than only answering the immediate inquiry.
