Payroll Validation · SMARTi

Company: SMARTi — employee, attendance and HR management software.

A feature for re-importing and validating payroll-data files, clearly displaying items, quantities, rates and amounts.

Result: 50% fewer errors in the process.
Expand for details
  • Need: Check outgoing payroll-data files and confirm that items and amounts are correct.
  • What I developed: A feature to re-import payroll data into a clear, understandable report.
  • Data checked: Payroll code, item, quantity and rate for each payroll line.
  • Challenge: Such files usually contain hundreds or thousands of salaries, making it difficult to understand what was generated.
  • Risk: Even a small error of a few tens of agorot can accumulate into hundreds of shekels.
  • Solution: Importing, linking and clearly displaying fields for comparison with the attendance report and verification of items, quantities, rates and all required payroll lines.
  • Result: Approximately 50% fewer payroll-transfer errors.
