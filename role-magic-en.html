01

Payroll Validation · SMARTi

Company: SMARTi — software solutions for workforce management, attendance and HR processes.

Developed a feature for re-importing and validating payroll data, clearly displaying payroll codes, items, quantities, rates and amounts.

Result: 50% reduction in payroll-transfer errors.
Expand for details

In my most recent role, I developed a feature that re-imported payroll data from a payroll export file into a readable report. This made it possible to identify export problems and verify that the correct payroll items and amounts were being transferred. The feature re-imported the payroll code, item, quantity and rate for every payroll line so the output could be validated.

Generating such a file often involves hundreds or thousands of salaries, making it difficult to know exactly what was produced. Even an error of a few tens of agorot can accumulate into hundreds of shekels. Because the original file was not practical to read directly, the data had to be imported, linked and displayed in an organized structure so it could be compared with the attendance report and the correct item, quantity, rate and required lines could be verified. The feature reduced payroll-transfer errors by approximately 50%.

Expand for details

Visual Vehicle Lot · Shlomo SIXT

Company: Shlomo SIXT — automotive services group covering vehicle rental, leasing and sales.

Managed an end-to-end project to create a visual model of a vehicle lot with vehicle information and history available by selecting each vehicle.

Expand for details

At Shlomo SIXT, I managed a visual vehicle-lot project that showed each vehicle's physical location in the lot. Each vehicle was represented as a button containing a summary of its details according to vehicle type. Selecting the vehicle displayed the full vehicle-card details and relevant information.

The challenge was to build the visual layout so it resembled the actual physical parking arrangement. Selecting a vehicle also provided its relevant history, enabling users to follow the vehicle as it was sent for repair and returned according to the required parameters.
